Inter 4.0 (regular, bold, italic, bold italic) from https://github.com/rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Inter-LICENSE.txt). Latin, Greek, Cyrillic; no Hebrew, Arabic or CJK.
DejaVu Sans (regular, bold, oblique, bold oblique), version 2.37, from the Bitstream Vera / DejaVu project. Free to use, copy and redistribute with the software (see LICENSE). Covers Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Hebrew, Arabic and more; no CJK (the CJK fallback still comes from the system).
Inter is the UI font; DejaVu Sans is its fallback (Hebrew, Arabic) and the sans font when the Inter files are missing.
